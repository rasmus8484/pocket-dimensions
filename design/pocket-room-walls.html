Pocket Room Walls

Standing inside a full 20 x 20 x 20 pocket room. The walls are the inside of the Tumbling Cube you stepped into, so the room reads as that small cube, only far bigger from within. Everything is full-bright with no shading, and a faint shimmer travels across the walls.

A · Cube Panels

Every wall block is one face of the cube: a stone frame, gold knobs where four blocks meet, a recessed ring and an end-portal starfield window. Floor and ceiling are the cube's rune plates. The shimmer lights the frames in a slow wave.

B · Open Void

The walls are almost all starfield, so the room seems to hang in space. Only the room's twelve edges are the cube's stone edges, with gold knobs at its eight corners, and a faint grid shows the blocks. An aurora wave rolls across the void.

C · The Great Cube

The whole room is one giant Tumbling Cube seen from inside: a stone frame around each wall, gold corner knobs, one continuous starfield window per wall, and the cube's rune spanning the whole floor and ceiling. The shimmer runs along the giant rune and sweeps across the windows.

D · Rune Cells

Bevelled stone tiles from the cube's plates, each with a small carved rune, and the starfield showing through thin cracks between them. The runes light up in a wave that travels across the room.

How it would be built

  • No shadows: the wall models switch shading and ambient occlusion off and give their faces full light emission, so nothing darkens them.
  • Starfield: an animated, tileable texture (stars drift and twinkle). It is close to the Tumbling Cube's end-portal windows but not the same effect, since plain block models can't use the end portal's render type.
  • Shimmer as one wave: each wall block gets a phase from its position when the room is built, and picks the matching offset of the animated texture, so the shimmer rolls across the walls instead of every block pulsing together. Very cheap: no renderers on the roughly 2,400 wall blocks.
  • C and the room edges in B need each block to know where it sits (edge, corner, which piece of the giant rune); that is a few block variants placed by the room generator.
