Siege Bars

Round two, built the way Cataclysm builds its boss bars: a thin ornate frame, a big emblem that breaks the bar's outline, and a dark outline around every piece. Each bar is a stack: a crest, a carved banner holding the flavour text, the bar itself, a plaque holding the numbers, and a pendant. The themed art sits behind every line of text, and both lines run the full width, left to right.

How the lines behave

  • Progress (the upper line) runs left to right in the siege colour, a slow wave flowing toward its bright head. Warded tints it toward the core's blue with a sheen; out of lapis it goes cold and still, and so do the glows.
  • Fuel (the lower line) is the siege block's lapis on the anchor side and the World Core's lapis on the core side. One slot per lapis while the max is 16 or less, one continuous line above that; the exact count always reads on the plaque.
  • Warded: while the World Core has lapis, a veined blue force field covers the progress line and the time on the plaque turns ward-blue behind a small shield, counting the slowed time. The progress keeps its siege colour underneath. With no lapis in the siege block, it goes dormant.
  • The plaque under the bar reads progress on the left, time left in the middle (or Warded, or Dormant), and lapis on the right.
  • Height is 48 px per bar against vanilla's 19. Say the word and I'll trim crest and pendant first; they're the cheapest rows to lose.
