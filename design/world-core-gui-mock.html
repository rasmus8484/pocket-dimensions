Relocate Hollowmere?

A new realm grows somewhere else. This one is lost forever:
  • every block built or mined
  • every chest and what is in it
  • the lapis in the ward
Players inside are sent back to the anchor. Your access list is kept.

The stone is the screen

  • Polished faces: wherever there is text, the rock flowed smooth and flat, like the tablets under the core's openings, so letters read against calm stone.
  • Four levels: the realm's name at double size; glowing rune light for live state; gold-inlaid headings for each part; dark engraved text for everything else.
  • Rune light: what matters glows: the realm's name, its state, the open seal, anything under your cursor. The glow follows the siege colour.
  • Lines of the anchors' runes are carved along the top and bottom of the slab, with light running through them slowly.
  • The ward is a small crystal-lined hollow in the rock; the vein beside it fills with crystal as you add lapis.
  • The relocate warning is a cracked tablet with its runes burning red.

Who sees what

  • Owner: all three seals, crowns managers, can relocate.
  • Managers: all three seals, add and remove ordinary players, rename. Crowns are read-only for them.
  • Ordinary players: only Overview. They can give lapis to the ward but not take it.

In game

  • The slab is one rock texture with a broken outline; carvings and runes are sprites drawn on it, so it stays crisp at every GUI scale. Text uses Minecraft's font with a one-pixel lit shadow under it.
