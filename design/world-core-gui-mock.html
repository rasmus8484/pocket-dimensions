Relocate Hollowmere?

A new realm is grown somewhere else. This one is lost forever:
  • every block built or mined
  • every chest and what is in it
  • the World Core's lapis
Players inside are sent back to the anchor. Your access list is kept.

Who sees what

  • Owner: all three tabs, can crown and uncrown managers, can relocate.
  • Managers: all three tabs, can add and remove ordinary players and rename the realm. Crowns are read-only for them and they can't remove each other.
  • Everyone else: crouch-clicking the core shows the sealed message, as now.

How it maps to the game

  • Panel, tabs and buttons: our own nine-slice sprites, so they stay crisp at every GUI scale.
  • Faces: the player's real skin face, the same one the tab list uses.
  • Your inventory only shows on Overview, where the lapis slot is.
  • The relocate button waits three seconds before it can be pressed.
  • Text uses Minecraft's own font in game.
