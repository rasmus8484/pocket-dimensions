Ward Sigil Studies

Rune-only ways to draw a realm's ward in its sky, seen from the ground the way a player sees them, with the mod's own rune sprites drawn as the game draws them. No strokes: every shape is traced by small runes, in layers at different heights, and runes may travel on curves. Pick a study, then try the siege states, where you stand ("Halfway out" shows the layers best) and how power leaves the beam.
